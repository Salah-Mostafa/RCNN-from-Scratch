
In transfer learning, we usually separate the feature extractor and the classifier or head. 
For R-CNN implementation we will use the base/backbone or feature ectractor and also the classifier layers of AlexNet. 
The classifier layers are the last three fully connected layers of AlexNet. 
In this implementation we will neglect the last layer of the classifier, 
since we will train our own SVM and Ridge regression models for object detection.
